## Engineering work in development

These initiatives are presented as **ongoing R&D**, not as published products or complete open-source releases. Selected architecture, test results, and demonstrations may be documented publicly as they mature.

- **Hailo Vision — Edge analytics:** Raspberry Pi 5 with Hailo-8 acceleration and camera-based detection, tracking, and local traffic measurements. Emphasis on offline-first operation and measured field performance.
- **RELOX — Precision synchronization:** GNSS-referenced LTC generation for multi-camera and multi-audio workflows, integrating timing electronics, firmware, and device-level validation.
- **DollyBot — Self-balancing motion platform:** ESP32-based balancing robot with motion planning, remote controls, and safety-focused iterative testing.
- **Modular mechanical systems:** Parametric CAD, 3D-printed mechanisms, positive-locking connectors, and functional prototypes designed for repeatable assembly.

## Industrial engineering background

My work spans the transition from **construction → pre-commissioning → commissioning → start-up**, including offshore platforms and complex industrial facilities. Areas of experience include:

- System completion, multidisciplinary interfaces, and operational readiness
- Mechanical and process commissioning; HVAC and rotating equipment

This background shapes how I approach software and hardware: **define the requirement, measure the behavior, diagnose the deviation, and verify the result.**
